Calm, careful dentistry for Ajmer since 2009

We offer general care, cosmetic dentistry, dental implants and smile makeovers from two clinics in Ajmer. Our focus is prevention, personal attention and fair prices, in a setting that eases dental anxiety.

In 2021 we became an NABH-accredited dental facility, which means our hygiene, safety and patient-care processes are independently audited.
